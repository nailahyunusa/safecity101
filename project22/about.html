About the Nigeria Police Force

The Nigeria Police Force (NPF) was formally established in 1930 following the merger of regional police units. It serves as the country’s main law enforcement body, with responsibilities ranging from crime prevention to investigation, protection of lives and property, and maintaining peace across Nigeria.

Kaduna State Command

The Kaduna State Police Command is one of the most strategic commands in Northern Nigeria due to its population and security challenges. Headquartered in Kaduna city, it oversees all 23 LGAs in the state and operates specialized units such as the Anti-Kidnapping Squad, State CID, and Tactical Response Teams.

The command focuses on tackling crimes like banditry, kidnapping, robbery, and urban criminal activity, while also working with communities through neighborhood policing and partnerships. With digital platforms like SafeCity, Kaduna State Command strengthens its capacity to respond swiftly to reports and improve public trust.
